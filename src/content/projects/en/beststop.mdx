## Problem

In large shopping centers, finding a spot takes time and causes congestion inside the parking lot itself.

## Solution

A system that uses artificial intelligence to analyze parking-lot cameras, compute how full each area is and recommend the best places to park in real time.

## Two interfaces

- **Drivers:** assisted navigation to a free spot.
- **Operators:** monitoring, operational control and decision support.

## Outcome

Presented at Fetin (Inatel's technology fair), designed to plug into cameras, sensors and information panels in real venues.
