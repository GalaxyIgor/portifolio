## Problem

I wanted to explore WebGL without leaving out people on modest devices or who prefer less motion.

## Solution

A navigable 3D gallery with React Three Fiber and an HTML/CSS version that kicks in automatically when 3D isn't a good idea.

## Technical decisions

- Procedural geometry instead of heavy models.
- Rendering pauses when the scene leaves the viewport.
- Checks for `prefers-reduced-motion` and WebGL support before loading Three.js.

## Outcome

The 3D experience shows up where it runs well, and everyone else gets a light version with the same content.
